Questions businesses may have

Does it cost my business anything to participate?

The proposed model has no upfront or recurring merchant participation fee. Any final program terms will be provided in writing before a business agrees.

Who decides what discount I offer?

You propose and control the offer, including eligible products or services, exclusions, and redemption conditions. The details must be agreed and clearly presented before publication.

Am I required to offer a discount on everything?

No. Offers can be limited to selected items, services, days, or other clearly stated conditions, subject to the final participation terms.

Am I committed indefinitely?

The intended approach is voluntary participation with clear written procedures for changing or ending an offer. Those procedures will be set out in the final agreement.

Is MemberShare accepting businesses now?

This is an early website prototype. The inquiry form helps demonstrate the process, but it is not yet connected to a live enrollment system.
